## Item 13.4 — proposed independent audit sink and operational controls

**DESIGN ONLY; NOT IMPLEMENTED OR TESTED.** An independent security team should own a separate append-only, retention-locked sink with a service identity that can append but cannot delete or alter previous events, and separate privileged readers; application DB owners and service roles must not administer its retention or keys. Use transactional outbox entries committed with each audited customer mutation, containing a unique event ID, tenant/entity IDs, actor or explicit unavailable marker, timestamp, operation, changed field names (no values), schema version and correlation ID. A separately administered shipper should deliver with authenticated encryption, durable retries, event-ID idempotency, monitored lag and dead-letter handling. Acknowledge only after durable storage; test crash/replay, duplicate, partial delivery and outage recovery. Review whether mutation must fail closed if the outbox cannot be written and availability implications.

A DB owner can disable triggers or manipulate an outbox; an external sink does **not** magically detect an event never emitted. Independently capture and alert on privileged DDL, trigger disablement, role/grant changes, database/audit logging interruptions, and unexpected sequence/CDC gaps through separately administered infrastructure; test what the provider can actually guarantee. Consider independently verified cryptographic chaining/signing with key separation, but do not claim nonrepudiation without an adversary and key model. Define security-approved retention periods, legal holds, tenant erasure treatment and minimization for event metadata and 010 PII request ledger; none are decided. Require periodic access/role reviews, restore-tested encrypted backups, segregated recovery keys, documented RPO/RTO and incident reconciliation of local versus remote event sets. No provider/cloud provisioned or sink activated.

Local cases remain **PASS synthetic** for runtime write denial and atomic rollback; owner-trigger disable is **FAIL tamper-proofness**. `tests/database/run-tests.mjs` test names are in the preceding table. Claims of production immutability, retention, independent ownership and restore capability are **BLOCKED**.
